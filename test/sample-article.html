City Libraries Stay Open Until Midnight

Public libraries in a mid-sized city will stay open until midnight on weekdays, starting next month. Officials say the experiment will last for six months before a final decision.

The city council approved the proposal after a survey showed that many residents, especially students and night-shift workers, wanted a quiet place to study after work. According to the survey, approximately sixty percent of young adults said their homes were too noisy.

Critics worry about the cost. Keeping buildings open longer means higher electricity bills and more staff, and the library budget is already small. Some librarians also raised concerns about security late at night.

Supporters argue that libraries are essential community infrastructure. They say the extra hours could reduce inequality, because not everyone can afford a private study café.

The city will publish attendance numbers every month. If fewer than two hundred people visit each night, the council may return to the old schedule.
